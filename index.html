$40M Retailer Loyalty Redesign: 15% to 55% Participation

Bajaj Electricals • Sales Strategy & Operations Lead • 200,000+ Retailers • 350+ Reps • 15 Regions

Owned end-to-end design and execution. National retailer participation sat at a sluggish 15% across 200,000+ retail partners. The organization assumed the incentive budget was uncompetitive. My root-cause diagnosis revealed the true friction was extreme operational complexity: 13 irregular 28-day cycles, opaque multi-variable formulas, and 100% reliance on sales reps for point statements. I redesigned the $40M architecture into a clean 12-month calendar with app-based self-tracking. We achieved our 18-month strategic goal in just 6 months — with zero incremental budget expansion.

15% → 55% participation in 6 mos +15% revenue uplift +25% SKU billing depth $0 budget expansion

Key Interventions & Methodology
  • Replaced 13 irregular cycles with a standardized 12-month calendar aligned to quarterly milestones.
  • Partnered with Product team to deploy app-based self-tracking, eliminating sales rep administrative bottlenecks.
  • Simplified qualifications to straightforward volume & product-mix targets verified via scenario modeling.
  • Led change management across 350+ sales reps with weekly regional adoption reviews.
Verified Measurable Outcomes
  • 15% to 55% retailer participation achieved in 6 months (18-month target).
  • Reactivated 35% of previously dormant targeted accounts.
  • Adopted as official national standard across all 15 regions.

04 —

Distributor ROI Optimization & Dual-Trigger Incentive Redesign

Bajaj Electricals • Sales Strategy & Operations Lead • 100 Distributors • 50 Reps • 97-Person Field Org

Owned end-to-end design and execution. Stagnant distributor ROI (18–20%) was being misdiagnosed as partner apathy. My forensic P&L audit uncovered that sales reps were incentivized exclusively on gross invoice value with zero accountability for cash collection. Distributor capital was locked up in market credit at 1.5 to 2.0x monthly sales. I presented a structural redesign to the VP of Sales: introducing a Dual-Trigger Incentive Structure that tied field payouts to timely cash collections.

+25–50% distributor ROI +55% payment collection speed -18% distributor churn +22% average order size
Read full case study →
Key Interventions & Methodology
  • Executed distributor P&L analysis revealing excessive credit exposure and low-margin product bias.
  • Built dual-trigger Excel compensation models: commission unlocked upon verified collections.
  • Operationalized real-time CRM collection workflows and trained 50 reps on profitable range-selling.
  • Scaled from a 2-region pilot to a 97-person national organization within 2 quarters.
Verified Measurable Outcomes
  • 25% to 50% ROI improvement lifting partner profitability into the healthy 24–30% band.
  • Market outstanding compressed from 2.0x down to 1.2x monthly sales.
  • 18% churn reduction, retaining crucial market distribution points.
