Case study, Bajaj Electricals

Crisis go-to-market during lockdown

During the 2020 lockdown I built a zero-cost digital ordering channel in days. It brought in $2M of revenue in 72 hours.

Retail stopped, $8M of stock frozen

In spring 2020, lockdowns froze the consumer durables trade. Field visits were prohibited. Distributor counters closed. Order-taking stopped overnight.

Over $8M of aging inventory sat in regional warehouses, with carrying costs rising daily. Budgets were frozen, so there was no marketing money.

Waiting meant write-downs and lost share. We needed a working go-to-market motion within 72 hours.

What I did

Custom software through central IT would have taken 4 to 6 months. We had days.

  1. Segmented micro-markets

    I matched 4,000+ accounts to local lockdown severity tiers. That isolated stores that were partly open or about to reopen.

  2. Repositioned the product

    Premium anti-bacterial and anti-viral fans had sold slowly because of price. We presented them as hygiene and air-circulation essentials for shops and clinics.

  3. Built a simple order funnel

    WhatsApp Business kits carried short demo clips and spec cards. A Google Forms order page fed distributor fulfillment queues.

  4. Mobilized the field team

    95 reps joined daily morning video briefings on remote selling, objection handling and digital order confirmation.

From emergency response to permanent capability

The campaign cleared a quarter of the aged inventory without discounting margins.

It also showed that tier-2 and tier-3 retailers will order through digital channels when the interface is familiar. Leadership kept the remote motion as a permanent national model. It lowered the cost to serve long-tail, rural accounts.

Before and after

Before

  • Sales halted, with reps idle at home
  • $8M of aging inventory adding carrying charges
  • No marketing budget
  • Uncertainty about retailer solvency and stock
  • Orders depended on physical rep visits

After

  • 95 reps selling remotely within 1 week
  • $2M revenue in 72 hours, with 25% of aged stock cleared
  • No added software or advertising spend
  • 4,000+ accounts engaged through digital ordering
  • Adopted as the remote-selling standard across 15 regions

Agility is not how fast you can buy expensive software. It is how quickly you combine the data you have with tools everyone already uses to fix an urgent balance-sheet problem.
